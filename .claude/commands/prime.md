Orient for the day. Load context, then surface only the live threads.

## Step 1: Load context

- `Context/about.md`, `Context/team.md`, `Context/voice.md`, `Context/email-priorities.md`
- `Context/deadlines-sa.md` — the statutory calendar

If `Context/` files are still `<placeholders>`, stop and offer `/setup`.

## Step 2: Scan

- `Deadlines/` tracker + monthly-close — anything due this week or overdue
- `Context/deadlines-sa.md` — any statutory deadline within the next 14 days
- `Committees/` — open action points she owns or is chasing
- `Reports/recurring/` — any report due this month not yet in `Reports/monthly/`
- `Team/` — anything a team member owes that's overdue

## Step 3: Brief

Tight, fragments, no padding:

```
## [Day] · [date]

⚠ [overdue or due-this-week statutory item, if any]

**Due this week**
- [item] — owner — due

**Open committee actions**
- [item] — owner — due

**Reports outstanding**
- [report] — due

**Team — behind**
- [person]: [item] — was due [date]
```

Omit any section that's empty. End with one line: the single most important thing to handle first.
