description: Team accountability — who owes what, what's overdue, chase drafts ready
---

Keep her team on track without her having to chase manually.

## Gather

1. `Context/team.md` and `Team/` — who's on the team and what each owns.
2. `Deadlines/` tracker, `Reports/recurring/`, `Committees/` — deliverables that have owners on her team.
3. Match deliverables to owners; find what's overdue or due soon.

## Output

```
## Team status — [date]

### Behind
- [person]: [deliverable] — was due [date]

### Due this week
- [person]: [deliverable] — due [date]

### Clear
- [person] — nothing outstanding
```

## Follow-ups

Offer to draft a chase message per person who's behind — in her voice, grouped so each person gets one message covering all their items. Keep it warm but clear: what's outstanding, when it was due, what she needs and by when.

## Rules

- Group by person, not by task — she manages people, not a task list.
- Be fair: if something's late because it's blocked on her or someone else, say so rather than putting it on the team member.
- Don't manufacture urgency. "Clear" is a valid and good status.
