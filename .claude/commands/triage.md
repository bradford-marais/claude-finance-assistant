---
description: Prioritise the inbox — what needs her now vs what can wait
---

Sort her email so she spends attention where it matters.

## Input

- If a Gmail/Outlook connector is set up, pull recent unread mail.
- Otherwise, ask her to paste the inbox (subject + sender + snippet is enough), or forward a block of emails.

## Before sorting

Read `Context/email-priorities.md` (VIPs, what's urgent vs not) and `Context/voice.md` (for reply drafts).

## Output

Bucket every email into one of these, most urgent first:

```
## 🔴 Now — needs her today
- [sender] · [subject] — why it's urgent

## 🟡 This week — important, not urgent
- [sender] · [subject] — what it needs

## 🟢 Quick — can be replied in one line (drafts below)
- [sender] · [subject]

## ⚪ Defer / delegate
- [sender] · [subject] — who could own it instead

## FYI — no action
- [sender] · [subject]
```

Then, for the 🟢 quick ones, write ready-to-send reply drafts in her voice.

## Rules

- Lean on `email-priorities.md` — VIP senders surface to the top regardless of subject.
- Anything from SARS, auditors, the bank, or the board defaults to at least 🟡.
- Don't over-escalate. If everything is urgent, nothing is — be honest about what can wait.
- Flag anything that looks like a deadline so it can go into `Deadlines/`.
